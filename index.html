ABOUT SKYWARDEN

Skywarden is an original armored-flight game. The pilot, the twenty-four SW-series frames, the gantry bay, the city grid and the ORIN and VELA assistants were designed for this game and are not based on any existing film, comic or character.

3D rendering uses the open-source three.js library. Spoken lines use your device's built-in speech voices; pick one in the menu.
